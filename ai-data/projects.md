# DejaVu

DejaVu is a memory game app by Vinesh Raju, live on the Google Play Store since 2015 (package: com.thevinesh.dejavu). It challenges players to remember and repeat patterns, and has been maintained and updated for over a decade. An iOS version is in review on the App Store.

- Store link (Android): https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.thevinesh.dejavu
- More details: https://thevinesh.com/builds/dejavu/

DejaVu is one of several apps Vinesh has shipped; see his builds page for Wack-A-Moji and Pulse.
